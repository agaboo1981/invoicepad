About Invoice Pad

Invoice Pad is a small toolkit for people who invoice their own work. It started as a free invoice generator. It now also includes a tax set-aside helper and a paid spreadsheet for keeping a 2027 income record.

The free tools run entirely in your browser. We do not create an account for you, and invoice drafts are not uploaded to a server.

The site is built for a global audience, with the United States and the United Kingdom as the first audiences for tax-planning copy. You can still use the invoice generator and a custom set-aside rate wherever you work.
