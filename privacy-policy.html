Overview

InvoicePad is operated by Joshua Adesina. This policy explains how the website handles information when you use the invoice generator and related pages.

Information we process

  • Invoice content you enter is stored locally in your browser and is not sent to our servers by default.
  • Referral parameters in URLs (for example ?ref=) may be stored in local browser storage to support user sharing attribution.
  • Basic web server logs may include IP address, browser type, and request metadata for reliability and security.

Advertising and cookies

InvoicePad may display Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show relevant advertising based on your visits. You can manage ad settings through Google Ads Settings and opt out via industry tools where available.

Your choices

  • You can clear local browser storage at any time to remove saved invoice drafts and referral data.
  • You can disable cookies in your browser, but some ad features may be affected.
