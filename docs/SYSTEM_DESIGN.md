# Invoice Pad system design

## Goals

Invoice Pad is a global, zero-cost tool site for freelancers and the self-employed. The United States and the United Kingdom are the first audiences, but every tool works anywhere.

1. Win search traffic with a free, fast, client-side invoice generator (PDF/print, localStorage, no account).
2. Keep visitors with a free tax set-aside / quarterly estimate helper. It is clearly an estimate, not tax advice.
3. Convert with a paid “2027 Freelancer Tax & Income Organizer” (Google Sheets template, $19). The Buy button is a placeholder until a payment link is supplied.

The site must stay free to host, require no backend of its own, and never invent demand, testimonials, or revenue.

## Users

- Freelancers, contractors, and sole traders who need a professional invoice quickly
- The same people who need a simple “how much should I set aside?” number during the year
- US 1099 / self-employed and UK self-assessment users in tax-planning season (especially Jan–Feb), plus anyone else who sets a custom rate

## Features

### Free invoice generator

- From/to details, invoice number, issue and due dates, currency
- Line items (description, quantity, unit price), percent discount, tax rate, tax-inclusive toggle, notes, payment details
- Optional logo (stored locally)
- Live paper preview, browser print / Save as PDF
- Autosave to `localStorage`, reset, works offline once loaded
- No sign-up, no server upload of invoice data

### Free tax set-aside helper

- Income, expenses, configurable set-aside rate
- Presets only (not legislation): US freelancer 30% quarterly, UK sole trader 25% with two instalments, and Custom (default 25%, quarterly)
- Outputs: profit, amount to set aside, leftover, per-period amount
- Prominent disclaimer: estimates, not tax advice; not a filing tool; rates are user-controlled

### Paid organizer

- Landing page for the 2027 Freelancer Tax & Income Organizer
- What the sheet covers (income, expenses, receipts log, set-aside, quarterly estimates)
- Price $19 USD, Buy button with a placeholder href until a Paystack or Gumroad (or similar) link is provided
- No false “in stock / download starts now” behaviour while the link is a placeholder

### Content and trust

- Home page that explains the three products
- Keyword landings that point at the real tools
- About, contact, privacy, terms, in actual HTML documents
- Honest copy; no fake reviews or usage counts

## Data flow

```
User input  →  pure calculation modules  →  preview / totals
                 ↓
           localStorage (optional autosave, this browser only)
                 ↓
           print stylesheet → browser “Save as PDF”
```

- Invoice and tax state never leaves the browser.
- The Buy button is a navigation to an external checkout. No card data is collected on this site.
- The Google Sheet itself is delivered by the payment provider after purchase (once the link exists), not generated by this app.

## Client-only vs backend

**Prefer static, zero-cost, no backend.** The product is a multi-page static site. There is no application API, no database, and no user accounts.

An optional tiny Node static server (`server.js`) may serve `dist/` and `/health` so a Node PaaS (including the current Pxxl-style host) can run the build. It must not implement product logic or 301 SEO pages into `/`.

## SEO strategy

- One real HTML document per URL (not a client-side router). Primary copy lives in the HTML.
- Unique title, description, and canonical on every page.
- Target queries: “free invoice generator”, “freelance invoice template”, “1099 tax organizer”, “self-employed tax spreadsheet 2027”, plus natural variants on the tool pages themselves.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` list only pages that exist.
- JSON-LD for the site and the free SoftwareApplication on the invoice tool; Product/Offer on the organizer (price visible, availability honest).
- Canonical host is configurable (`VITE_SITE_URL`, default `https://invoicepad.shop`). Confirm with the owner before treating `invoicepad.shop` vs `invoicepad.pxxl.click` as the public domain.
- Fast first load, indexable without hanging on a large JS bundle. Tools may enhance with JavaScript; pages remain readable if script is slow.

## Payments

- One paid SKU: 2027 Freelancer Tax & Income Organizer, $19.
- Checkout is off-site. The Buy href is `VITE_PAYMENT_URL` or a clearly labelled placeholder (`#payment-link-pending`).
- Do not accept offers, take cards, or claim a sale without a verified payment.

## Analytics

- None by default. No analytics SDK, no ad network, no third-party tracker on the first ship.
- If analytics are added later, use a privacy-friendly, free, cookieless option (for example a self-hosted or GDPR-minimal tool) and disclose it in the privacy policy. Not in scope for this rebuild.
