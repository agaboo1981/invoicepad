Freelance invoice template

A clear invoice gets you paid faster. Use this page as a checklist, then fill the same fields in the free generator and save a PDF.

Open the freelance invoice template

Every freelance invoice should name

  • Your name or trading name, and how to contact you
  • The client’s name and address
  • A unique invoice number, the issue date, and the due date
  • Each piece of work, with quantity or hours and a unit price
  • Tax, if you charge it, and whether prices already include it
  • How to pay you, and any terms you have already agreed

The generator on this site is a live template: the paper preview is the document you send. It is not a locked layout from a word processor, and you do not need an account to use it.
