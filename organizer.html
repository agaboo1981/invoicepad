What is in the sheet

Seven tabs:

  • Start Here — how to use the workbook, and a reminder that this is an organizing tool, not tax advice
  • Income — client, date, invoice number, and amount
  • Expenses — costs you can sort by category
  • Mileage — trips and a rate you enter for your country and year
  • Receipts — a checklist of supporting documents
  • Quarterly Set-Aside — a rate you choose and planning columns for the year
  • Summary — yearly totals calculated from the other tabs

Works in Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, and Apple Numbers. Use one book currency and convert other payments into that currency when you log them.

The sheet helps you stay organized. It is not tax advice and it does not file anything for you.
