Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

What runs in your browser

The invoice generator and tax helper save drafts in your browser’s local storage on this device. That data is not sent to Invoice Pad. Clearing your browser data deletes the draft. We cannot recover it.

What we collect

This site does not require an account and does not include an analytics or advertising script in this version. Your web host may keep standard server logs such as IP address, date, and the page requested.

Payments

The 2027 Freelancer Tax & Income Organizer is a digital product sold and delivered through Gumroad. Checkout and the instant download happen on Gumroad’s site. You can pay there with a card or PayPal. Gumroad’s privacy policy applies to that payment and delivery. We never ask for your card number on Invoice Pad.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of that request.
