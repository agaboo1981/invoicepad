Invoice Template Guide

Use this guide to structure invoices that are easy for clients to review and fast for you to send.

Start With Free Template

What every invoice template should include

  1. Seller and client billing details
  2. Invoice number and payment deadline
  3. Clear line-item descriptions and quantities
  4. Tax and discount breakdown
  5. Total due and payment instructions

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Missing due dates or unclear payment terms
  • Vague line item descriptions
  • No tax explanation when tax is applied
