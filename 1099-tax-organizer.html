1099 tax organizer

If you work in the United States as a contractor, a simple organizer is often more useful than a pile of invoices at tax time. Invoice Pad gives you a free estimate tool now and a 2027 spreadsheet you can buy if you want a full-year record.

Estimates, not tax advice. This page does not calculate your federal or state return, self-employment tax, or quarterly payment vouchers.

Estimate what to set aside See the 2027 organizer

What to keep through the year

  • Income by client and date, including 1099-NEC amounts when they arrive
  • Business expenses and a place to note a receipt
  • A rate you choose for money to reserve, reviewed with your own adviser if you have one
  • A quarterly view so cash is ready before payment deadlines

The free helper is for a single running estimate. The paid sheet is for the year-long log.
