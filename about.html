About InvoicePad

InvoicePad is a free invoice generator created to help freelancers and small businesses produce clean, professional invoices without unnecessary complexity.

Who built InvoicePad

InvoicePad was built by Joshua Adesina, an independent developer. The mission is to provide a fast, reliable, and accessible invoicing tool that works globally.

What the tool does

  • Create invoices in minutes
  • Add line items, taxes, discounts, and notes
  • Generate print-ready PDF invoices
  • Keep drafts in local browser storage

Purpose and audience

InvoicePad is designed for freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small business owners who need a free invoice maker with practical features and a simple interface. It also serves users looking for a freelancer invoice tool Nigeria and beyond.
