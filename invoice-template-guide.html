What every invoice needs

  1. Seller and client details
  2. Invoice number and due date
  3. Clear line items with rates
  4. Tax/discount breakdown
  5. Total due and payment method

Common mistakes

  • Missing payment terms
  • Unclear line-item descriptions
  • No tax explanation
  • No contact details
